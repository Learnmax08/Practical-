සිද්ධාන්ත (Theory)
  • 🔹 අන්තරාලය (Pitch): $0.5\text{ mm}$
  • 🔹 කුඩාම මිනුම (Least Count):
    $$\text{LC} = \frac{\text{Pitch}}{\text{Divisions}} = \frac{0.5\text{ mm}}{50} = 0.01\text{ mm}$$
  • 🔹 දෝෂ ශෝධනය:
    $$\text{Corrected Reading} = \text{Observed Reading} - (\pm\text{Zero Error})$$
